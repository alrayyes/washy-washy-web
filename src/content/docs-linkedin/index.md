Big news (I know, I know, everyone says that — this one's actually big):
washy washy turns a laundry chart into something you can actually read
standing in front of the machine. A mobile-first, stakeholder-ready page,
filterable by cut and by deliverable. There's a separate
[CLI](https://github.com/alrayyes/washy-washy-cli) — shoutout to that
product line — that reads the same chart and machine description and
renders it as a PDF instead. This page covers the web platform only; the
CLI has its own README for installing and running it.

Here's where to start, depending on what you're trying to unlock:

- **[The roadmap and org chart](/linkedin/docs/chart-and-machine/)** —
  every field on your deliverable, how the org chart describes your washer
  and iron, and how the compatibility matrix decides what can co-locate in
  one drum.
- **[Using the platform](/linkedin/docs/web-app/)** — the filters on the
  front page, what persists between sessions, and how onboarding a config
  here relates to the bundled example.
- **[AI-assisted onboarding](/linkedin/docs/ai-prompt/)** — paste a
  copy-pasteable prompt and photos of your washer and iron, and get back a
  config file this platform can ingest.
